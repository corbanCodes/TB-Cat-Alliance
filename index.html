How it works

No printing bills, no postage, no EDDM permits. Digital pages, real local reach.

STEP 01

Send us your ad

Email your artwork or flyer — or tell us your offer and we'll lay the page out for you. Full pages, half pages, and two premium covers every issue.

STEP 02

We publish monthly

A fresh issue drops on the first of every month — a 3D flipbook readers browse on any phone or computer, shared across our community pages.

STEP 03

The community flips & saves

Every advertiser gets a QR window sticker so your own customers become readers — and every reader sees your ad. Deals get clipped, registers ring.

"Junk mail gets tossed. A fun little flipbook gets flipped."

Florida Dealz started the old-fashioned way — a printed community deal book, delivered by hand with flyers and a smile. Print worked, but printing, postage, and mailing fees made every page expensive.

Going digital changes the math: no print bill means lower ad prices for businesses, a fresh issue every month, and a book the whole county can flip through on any phone. Same community feel — minus the postage. (And yes, the cat stays. Every deal book needs a mascot.)

— Florida Dealz, Tampa Bay
